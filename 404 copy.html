Learn More about 1 Finance Calculators
The maths behind personal finance can be confusing. That’s why we have created a set of user-friendly financial calculators and tools to simplify these calculations for you. Whether you're deciding between the old tax regime and the new tax regime, calculating the commission you are paying on your insurance or mutual fund, determining how much corpus you need for retirement, calculating the benefit of loan refinancing, or evaluating the overlap in your mutual funds, our tools are here to help you make smarter decisions with confidence.
Income Tax Calculators
Plan your taxes more effectively with our easy-to-use Income Tax Calculators.
HRA Exemption Calculator
An HRA (House Rent Allowance) Exemption Calculator is a tool designed to help individuals determine the amount of HRA that is exempt from tax under Indian tax laws.
Old vs. New Income Tax Calculator
The Old vs New Income Tax Calculator is a tool designed to help taxpayers compare their tax liabilities under the old and new tax regimes ensuring optimal tax savings and compliance.
Insurance Calculators
Gain clarity on your policy's value and costs with our Insurance Calculators, making it easier to manage and optimise your insurance plans.
Surrender Value Calculator
Surrender Value Calculator provides a quick and easy way to estimate the surrender value of your traditional life insurance policies. Simply put, it helps you determine how much you'll receive if you decide to surrender your endowment policy.
Insurance Commission Analyser
An Insurance Commission Analyser is a tool specifically designed to estimate the commissions you've paid on your insurance policies, particularly life and health insurance.
Investment Calculators
Analyse and optimise your investments with our calculators for long-term growth.
Mutual Fund Portfolio Overlap
A Mutual Fund Portfolio Overlap Calculator helps you identify how much overlap exists between the holdings in your different mutual funds.
Increasing SIP Contribution Calculator
An Increasing SIP Contribution Calculator helps you understand how gradually increasing your investment contributions can impact your long-term financial growth. This calculator factors in your initial investment, annual contribution growth rate, and expected return to estimate future growth.
Mutual Fund Commission Analyser
A Mutual Fund Commission Analyser is a tool that helps you estimate the commissions you've paid on your mutual fund investments.
Loan & Debt Calculators
Manage your loans effectively with our Loan & Debt Calculators.
Loan Refinance Calculator
A Loan Refinance Calculator is a tool that helps you estimate the potential benefits of refinancing an existing loan. It does this by comparing the terms of your current loan with the terms of a potential new loan.
Retirement Calculators
Make informed decisions around your retirement by using our retirement calculators.
NPS Calculator
An NPS (National Pension System) Calculator helps users estimate the potential corpus they can accumulate under the National Pension System.
Retirement Corpus Calculator
A Retirement Corpus Calculator is a tool designed to help you estimate the amount of money you will need to accumulate by the time you retire to maintain your desired lifestyle.
Here at 1 Finance, we understand that solid financial planning is crucial for your well-being. Whether you're looking at insurance, mutual funds, or sorting out your taxes, our calculators are here to help. They're designed to make it easier for you to save on taxes, understand your investments, and make smart decisions, all to help you build a secure financial future.
